[YOUR_LEGAL_NAME_OR_COMPANY] (“we”, “us”) provides the 1Task mobile application, a workspace for companies and teams to manage projects, tasks, routine work, comments, and reports. This policy explains what information the app handles and how you can ask us to delete it.

1. Information you give us

  • Account. First name, last name, email address, password, and company name when you register. Passwords are sent to our server over HTTPS and are not stored in the app.
  • Google sign-in. If you choose Google, the app requests your Google email and basic profile. Google returns an authorization code, which our server exchanges for a 1Task session. We never receive your Google password. Google’s handling of that sign-in is described in Google’s Privacy Policy.
  • Profile. Optional profile photo, job title, country, city, state, phone numbers with country code, and links you add (such as a social profile).
  • Work content. Projects, one-time tasks, routine tasks, descriptions, comments, replies, reports, departments, roles, and files or images you attach.
  • Team. Email addresses you type when inviting someone to your company, plus names, roles, and departments of people in that workspace.
  • Subscription. The plan you select. Card and payment details are entered on the checkout page opened inside the app. That page is operated by our payment provider. 1Task does not store your full card number.

2. Information collected automatically

  • Session token. After login we store an access token in the device’s secure storage so you stay signed in. It is sent with requests to our API.
  • Time zone. Used only on the device to schedule local reminders.
  • Local app data. A small amount of task-tracking state is kept on the device with the system preferences store.

The current app does not use advertising SDKs, analytics SDKs, or crash-reporting SDKs. It does not read your GPS location, camera, microphone, SMS, or address book. Photos and files are accessed only after you pick them from your gallery or file picker. Reminders are local notifications on your device.

3. How we use information

  • Create and secure your account and company workspace.
  • Show tasks, comments, files, roles, and reports to people in your company who are allowed to see them.
  • Send invitations and password-reset messages to the email address you provide.
  • Show your profile to teammates and let you update it.
  • Schedule reminders you set, and process the subscription plan you choose.
  • Operate, protect, and troubleshoot the service, and meet legal duties.

4. Who can see it

Task content, comments, files, and profile details are visible to authorized users in the same company workspace, according to the roles your company assigns. We do not sell personal information.

We share information only with:

  • Our hosting provider, which runs the 1Task API that stores account and workspace data.
  • Google, when you use Google sign-in.
  • Our payment provider, when you pay for a subscription. The provider receives the payment details you enter on its page and the information needed to complete the charge.
  • Authorities, when the law requires us to disclose information.

5. Permissions

  • Internet — to reach the 1Task service.
  • Notifications, vibration, exact alarms, and boot completed — to deliver and restore local reminders.
  • Photos and files — to attach items you select and to open files you download from a task or comment.

6. How long we keep it

We keep account and workspace data for as long as the account or company workspace is active. Local tokens and preferences stay on the device until you log out, clear the app’s storage, or uninstall it. Server data is deleted or anonymized after we complete a verified deletion request, except for records we must keep for legal, security, or billing reasons.

7. Your choices and account deletion

You can update your profile inside the app. You can refuse notifications and photo access in the system settings; some features will then be unavailable.

To delete your account and the personal data tied to it, email [YOUR_EMAIL] from the address on the account. Include the account email and the company name. We will confirm and delete the account within 30 days, unless a longer period is required by law. Content you created inside a company workspace may remain available to that company if the company still needs it to operate. Deleting the app from your phone does not delete the server account.

You may also email us to access, correct, or export the personal information we hold about you.

8. Children

1Task is a workplace tool and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child gave us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

9. Security

Traffic between the app and our API uses HTTPS. The session token is stored in secure device storage. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

10. International processing

Your information is processed on our servers, which may be outside your country. Where the GDPR or a similar law applies, we process account and workspace data to perform our contract with you, and security or legal records on the basis of legitimate interests or legal obligation. You may lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

11. Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the effective date. Continued use of 1Task after the update means you accept the revised policy.
